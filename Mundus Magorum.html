Mundus Magorum

What is Mundus Magorum?

In this world, magic is abundant.
Humans and demons coexist.
Those who are skilled with magic become known as Magicians.
These are the stories of those Magicians, who they are, who they cross paths with, the places they live, and the adventures they embark on.

For a few months I've been working on Mundus Magorum as a background pet project. It's mostly for fun and to practice my own worldbuilding, but I figured that I could use it as a basis to try out some other ideas and gain experience. Essentially, it's my way of practicing my art and storytelling across different multimedia forms before I dive headfirst into more ambitious ideas in the future.

Things are barebones here for now, and there are a but the goal is that as time goes on I'll keep coming back to update this website, and the hope is that one day I'll be able to link it with a larger website that hosts all my projects under one hub.
